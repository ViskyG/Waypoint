## Waypoint map-to-evidence conformance
At the requested boundary compare the accepted graph/version with its status,
criterion receipts, scope declarations and actual executed scenario evidence. Check
binding coverage for each executor, declaration-before-work and release-at-handoff;
empty chats, an active declared scope and done are distinct observations. Preserve
native attribution. Do not guess missing historical links or infer physical absence
only because an availability receipt arrived late.

For each claim state expected behavior, observed behavior, evidence level, run/capture
basis, affected revision/workspace, consequence and concrete cause or uncertainty.
Verify referenced artifact identity where available. A mutable file at a historical
path or a writer-supplied current label does not prove fresh current-code validation.
Treat retrospective criterion registration explicitly; 47 green receipts cannot by
themselves prove all product scenarios passed. Separate old failed attempts, actual
repairs, synthetic reproductions and real-source integration.

Reopen or route a linked -FIX when an accepted requirement is demonstrably missing,
a required claimed check was not run, evidence materially contradicts completion or
a current required artifact is unavailable. Use an unverified finding when evidence
is insufficient, not a fabricated failure; optional improvements stay optional.
Preserve valid passes, original receipts and unexplained historical gaps. Plan the
smallest targeted proof for the repair; do not rerun every suite or rewrite the plan
just to remove a warning. No new execution gate or publication permission follows.
